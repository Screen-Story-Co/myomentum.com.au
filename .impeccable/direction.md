# Relaunch — Direction Derivation (yiapanis.co)

Mode: **Experience** — the visitor is inside the work itself. Portfolio of a creator.
Surface: the whole site, replacing the incumbent world (redesign, not refinement).

**Product truth (from PRODUCT.md):** one creator, two disciplines — filmmaker and
creative technologist. Audience: film industry *and* people assessing technical work.
Jobs: credibility + showcase. Crossover is latent, not yet over-claimed.

**The category rut (kept out):** the dark film-portfolio template — near-black ground,
one gold accent, "Director & Screenwriter", full-bleed stills, a filmstrip divider.
That is precisely the incumbent. Its predictable opposite — the bright minimal
developer portfolio with a project grid — is the other rut.

---

## The seven grounded directions

### 1. The contact sheet
A photographer's proof sheet: a grid of every frame on the roll, the chosen ones
circled in grease pencil. Analogue paper family.
*Carries the mechanism:* a body of work shown as a whole roll with selections marked —
honest about volume, decisive about the keepers.
*First surface:* the homepage IS the sheet; selections enlarge in place.

### 2. The stripboard
A production stripboard — coloured paper strips pinned to a board, one per scene,
rearranged until the schedule works. Analogue paper family.
*Carries:* work-in-development as material you physically reorder. Slate as plan, not pitch.
*First surface:* the development slate as a live stripboard.

### 3. The projection booth
The booth above a cinema: reels, the port window, the beam cutting smoke. Architecture family.
*Carries:* the moment before the image — craft, warmth, and the projectionist's anonymity.
*First surface:* a dark room where work plays.

### 4. The screenplay page
The page itself: Courier, scene headings, action blocks, dialogue indents. Paper family.
*Carries:* the writer's craft as the literal page — the discipline behind the films.
*First surface:* the bio as a screenplay page, the career in sluglines.

### 5. The commit log
A version-control history: diffs, branches, merge commits, tags. Software family.
*Carries:* the technical practice as a body of work with real provenance and dates.
*First surface:* the open-source/agent work as a browsable history.

### 6. The call sheet
The daily call sheet — crew list, schedule, locations, times, weather, notes. Paper family.
*Carries:* production as logistics; serious, operational, unglamorous, credible.
*First surface:* the profile as a call sheet — who, what, when.

### 7. The ledger — a release log bound like a production book  ← **ASSIGNED (#7)**
A production book that logs *both* kinds of work as entries in one bound ledger:
films on the recto, software releases on the verso, each with a date, a type mark,
a status, and a short note. The binding is the whole point — one practice, two columns,
one continuous record. Ledger/clerk-book family.
*Carries the mechanism:* the crossover is not asserted, it is *logged*. Two disciplines
share one spine and one date sequence, so the connection reads as fact rather than claim.
*First surface:* the homepage as the open ledger — recent entries on both sides,
the spine down the middle.
*Signature interaction:* the ledger pages turn; a filter narrows to one column without
tearing the book in half.
*Cross-surface reach:* the same entry grammar extends to any future surface — a film
detail is one entry expanded; a repo is another.
*Honest risk:* "two columns" can read as a gimmick if the entries are thin. The binding
must carry real, dated, verifiable work or it is decoration.

## Challengers dealt (catalog)

- **luxury-fashion-flagship** — monumental type, vast negative space, slow full-bleed
  imagery. Strong audience identification for the film side; weaker for the technical side.
- **operate-a-cutting-bench-select-rail** — the cutting bench as a perforated rail;
  grease crosses, tape flags. Very close to direction 2/7 materially; strongest product
  clarity for a filmmaker.
- **cyworld-minihompy-room** — an isometric furnishable room as identity. High novelty,
  weak product clarity here.
- **moon-shadow-bazaar**, **rain-night-cityscape**, others — world-rich, product-thin
  for this brief.

## Verdicts (weighed on audience identification × product clarity)

- cutting-bench-select-rail — **competitive**. Holds product clarity. Its discipline the
  assigned direction lacks: *state as a mark rather than a hue* — grease cross done, flag
  where you stopped. Raise: the ledger adopts mark-based state (struck-through, taped,
  initialled) instead of colour-coding status.
- luxury-fashion-flagship — **declined**. Its discipline: total commitment to negative
  space, letting one thing fill the frame. Raise: each ledger entry gets room to breathe
  rather than a dense table row.
- minihompy-room — **declined**. Its discipline: identity as something you furnish.
  No honest raise for this product; noted and dropped.

**Raises written into the assigned direction:** mark-based state (donor:
cutting-bench-select-rail) · negative-space commitment per entry (donor:
luxury-fashion-flagship).

## IMPECCABLE'S PICK (not the assigned direction)

**The contact sheet (1).** Honest risk: it is the most familiar of the seven for a
creative portfolio — close to where most runs in this category land.
