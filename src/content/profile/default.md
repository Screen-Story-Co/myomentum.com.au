<p>Myomentum is the clinical myotherapy practice of <strong>Phil Papadopoulos</strong> — Bachelor of Health Science (Clinical Myotherapy), Southern School of Natural Therapies (2015), Diploma in FIFA Football Medicine (2021), Certified Human Movement Specialist (2024).</p>

<p>Phil's interest in musculoskeletal care started as a young athlete living through his own costly, acute and long-term injuries. That experience became a practice: helping people living with pain rise above and beyond. Since 2011 he has worked with semi-professional soccer in the Victorian NPL and with young athletes at the National Futsal Championships (2017–), and in clinic with elite athletes, Ironman competitors, award-winning bodybuilders, kickboxers, an Olympic Gold & World-title boxer, Muay Thai Intercontinental Champion, MMA fighters, triathletes, A-League / A-League Women and Arsenal FC Women players, professional dancers, circus and performing-arts performers, and Wallabies/Rugby Union players.</p>

<p>Member, Myotherapy Association Australia. Works alongside sports physios, myotherapists, osteopaths, exercise physiologists and podiatrists.</p>
